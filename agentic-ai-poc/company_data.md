# Acme Technologies - Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

- Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per calendar year
- Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year
- Earned Leave (EL): 20 days per calendar year
- Unused Casual Leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year.

---

## Working Hours

- Office Days: Monday to Friday
- Office Timings: 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM
- Employees are expected to work 8 hours per day.

---

## Work From Home Policy

- Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week.
- Work From Home requests require manager approval.
- Employees must be available during core working hours.

---

## Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim reimbursement for:

- Internet expenses up to ₹1,500 per month
- Learning & Development courses up to ₹20,000 per year
- Travel expenses for approved business trips
